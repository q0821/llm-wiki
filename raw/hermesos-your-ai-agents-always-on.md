Common questions

## Straight answers.

#### Is my API key safe?

Yes. Encrypted at rest, injected at deploy via environment variables. We never proxy or log your AI requests.

#### Will updates break my setup?

No. Every update tested against container configs before it ships. Daily backups mean you're never more than 24 hours from a clean restore.

#### How is this different from OpenClaw?

OpenClaw is an excellent open-source desktop framework. HermesOS is a fully managed, production-grade cloud environment. Hermes Agent has more stable memory, higher reliability, and updates that don't break your existing features.

#### What if my agent crashes?

It restarts automatically. Health, logs, and resource usage always visible in the dashboard.

#### Can I run multiple agents on one plan?

Yes — unlimited profiles per instance. Free runs 1 active agent, Pro runs 3, Power has no concurrent cap. Your compute pool is the only real limit.

#### Which AI providers are supported?

OpenRouter, OpenAI, and Anthropic. OpenRouter alone gives you hundreds of models on a single key.

#### Do I need to use $HermesOS to access the platform?

No. Free tier doesn't require it. Pro and Power can be paid with card. The token gives you discounts and a third payment path for people who want it.

#### What's coming next?

Operator packs (pre-built agent templates) ship in the next few weeks. Marketplace, agent endpoints, and Hive Mind follow. The roadmap is on hermesos.cloud/roadmap.
